Call for Contributed Talks and Posters

We invite researchers to contribute to FMF200 through contributed talks and poster presentations.

Submissions provide an opportunity to share research, exchange ideas and engage with the mathematical finance community.

Contributed Talks

Researchers interested in presenting their work as a contributed talk are invited to submit a proposal for consideration.

Priority consideration for contributed talks will be given to postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty.

Poster Presentations

Researchers are invited to submit proposals for poster presentations showcasing their work in mathematical finance.

Submission Information

Researchers interested in presenting a contributed talk, poster or both will be asked to provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Institutional affiliation
  • Position
  • Preferred presentation type
  • Presentation title
  • Abstract
  • Collaborators, if applicable
  • arXiv, publication or related research link, if available

Selection priority: Postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty will receive priority consideration for contributed talks.

Submission details coming soon. The submission form and relevant deadlines will be published here once confirmed.
