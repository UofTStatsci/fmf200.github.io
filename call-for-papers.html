FMF200 | May 26–28, 2027

Call for Papers

The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200 invites researchers to submit proposals for contributed talks and poster presentations.

Taking place May 26–28, 2027, at the University of Toronto, FMF200 brings together the mathematical finance community to share research, exchange ideas and discuss emerging challenges in the field.

Submission deadline: December 20, 2026.

Researchers interested in presenting a contributed talk, poster or both are invited to submit their proposals by this date.

Presentation Opportunities

Researchers are invited to submit proposals for one or both of the following presentation formats.

Contributed Talks

Researchers interested in presenting their work as a contributed talk are invited to submit a proposal for consideration.

Priority consideration for contributed talks will be given to postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty.

Poster Presentations

Researchers are invited to submit proposals for poster presentations showcasing their work in mathematical finance.

Submission Information

To submit a proposal, applicants will be asked to provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Institutional affiliation
  • Academic or professional position
  • Preferred presentation format: contributed talk, poster or both
  • Presentation title
  • Abstract
  • Collaborators, if applicable
  • arXiv, publication or related research link, if available

Selection Priority

Postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty will receive priority consideration for contributed talks.
