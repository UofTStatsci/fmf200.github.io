About the Conference

The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200 brings together the mathematical finance community at the University of Toronto from May 26–28, 2027.

Conference Overview

Held in celebration of the University of Toronto's bicentennial, FMF200 provides a forum for researchers and members of the mathematical finance community to share research, exchange ideas and discuss the emerging challenges in the field.

The three-day conference will feature plenary and invited speakers, alongside opportunities for contributed talks and poster presentations.

FMF200 brings together perspectives from across mathematical finance, creating opportunities for academic exchange and discussion of new research and developments.
