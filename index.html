About FMF200

Exploring the future of mathematical finance

The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200 brings together researchers, practitioners, alumni and members of the University of Toronto community to explore the evolving landscape of mathematical finance.

Hosted by the Department of Statistical Sciences, the conference provides a forum for new research, emerging ideas and conversations about the future of the field.

U of T at 200

Two centuries of discovery

FMF200 is part of the University of Toronto's bicentennial celebration, recognizing 200 years of scholarship, discovery and impact.

The conference provides an opportunity to reflect on the University's contributions to mathematical finance while looking ahead to the ideas and people shaping its future.
