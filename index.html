About FMF200

Exploring the future of mathematical finance

The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200 brings together the mathematical finance community at the University of Toronto from May 26–28, 2027.

Held in celebration of the University of Toronto's bicentennial, the three-day conference will feature plenary and invited speakers, providing opportunities for research exchange and discussion of the emerging challenges in mathematical finance.

Call for Contributions

Share your research at FMF200

FMF200 invites researchers to submit proposals for contributed talks and poster presentations.

We welcome submissions addressing research and emerging challenges in mathematical finance.

Postdoctoral researchers and early-career faculty will receive priority consideration for contributed talks.

Researchers may submit proposals for a contributed talk, poster presentation or both.
