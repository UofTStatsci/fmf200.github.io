About FMF200

Exploring the future of mathematical finance

The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200 brings together the mathematical finance community at the University of Toronto.

Taking place May 26–28, 2027, the three-day conference will feature plenary and invited speakers and opportunities for research exchange and discussion.
